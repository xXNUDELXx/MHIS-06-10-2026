Let's Meet the Parrot!

Today we learn about a colorful bird that can talk!

Teacher idea: start by squawking "Hello!" and ask, "Who can say hello like a parrot?"

Parrot Fun Facts 🌈

  • 🗣️ Some parrots can copy people's words!
  • 🌴 Many live in warm, green forests.
  • 🥜 They eat seeds, nuts, and fruit.
  • 👨‍👩‍👧 Parrots love to live in big groups.
Activity: kids pick a fruit card and "feed" the parrot puppet.
🥜

The Beak

A parrot's beak is strong and curved, like a hook.

It cracks nuts and helps the parrot climb!

Activity: pinch with two fingers like a beak and "crack" a pretend nut.
🪽

Wings and Tail

Wings flap up and down so the parrot can fly high.

The long tail helps it steer, like a boat's rudder.

Activity: flap your arms like wings, then wiggle your "tail" to turn left and right!
👁️

The Eyes

Parrot eyes are on the sides of the head.

That lets them see almost all around, even behind!

Game: hide a toy parrot in the room. Kids spot it with their "parrot eyes."
🦶

The Claws

Parrot feet have 4 toes: 2 point forward, 2 point back.

That makes a super strong grip on branches!

Game: "Name the body part!" Teacher points to beak, wing, tail, eye, or claws, and kids shout it out. 🎉
